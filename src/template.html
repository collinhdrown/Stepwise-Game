    How to play

    Turn the current word into a new one by rearranging its letters or bringing in letters from the bank. Every step must make a real word of 3 or more letters that isn't already on your ladder.

    Moves

    • Add. Tap a bank letter, then a gap in the word. A new letter refills the bank.
    • Remove. Tap a letter in the word, then Remove. It doesn't go back to the bank.
    • Shift. Tap a letter in the word, then the gap you want it to move to.
    • Swap. Tap a letter in the word, then another letter to trade places.
    • Replace. Tap a letter in the word, then a bank letter to put in its place (or the other way round). A new letter refills the bank.

    Scoring

    • Word scores are determined by their letter values, creating longer words and using rarer letters will net you more points.
    • Category bonus. A word whose meaning fits the active bonus category scores 20 more, and a new category appears.
    • Toolkit bonus. Each kind of move lights up its icon. Light all five and you score 10 more, then the icons reset so you can earn it again.
    • Long word bonus. A 6-letter word scores 6 more and a 7-letter word 7 more. Longer words score their length.
    • Classic ends after 25 steps. Free play ends when no move is possible and no powers or undo can help.
